The Uma Musume Carat Calculator is a free, unofficial planning tool for players of Cygames' *Uma Musume Pretty Derby*. It answers a question that is hard to work out in your head: **will I be able to afford the banner I want?**

## What it does

Carats come from dozens of sources, each on its own schedule: daily logins, Team Trials on Mondays, club rank on the 1st, Champions Meeting and League of Heroes on the day each event ends, plus every event, login bonus and mission the game runs. Banners come and go on a two-week cycle that lines up with none of it.

You tell the calculator what you have right now and which income applies to your account. It walks the calendar forward day by day and shows what you will have on the day each banner ends, so you know before you spend. It also plans step-up banners, tracks which characters and support cards a selector ticket can still reach, and shows the upcoming banner and event schedule on a timeline.

## Where the numbers come from

Banner dates, event rewards and income rates are maintained here by hand and updated as the game announces them. The underlying methodology follows [Henry's resource spreadsheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/100t3hnYl5Qm2UR8RtPlH-8Xd9KQbBlxEdXUOIR4d394/), the community reference this site grew out of. Recent data and feature changes are listed on the [Changelog](/changelog), the [FAQ](/faq) explains each income source, and the [carat income guide](/guides/carat-income) walks through the projection step by step.

Everything the site produces is an **estimate**. Reward amounts and schedules get announced late or change, and one toggle set differently from how you play will move the total. Treat a projection as a forecast, not a promise.

## Who makes it

It is a small fan project, run alongside the [Henry Handsome Derby](https://www.youtube.com/@HenryHandsomeDerby) YouTube channel and the spreadsheet above. There is no company behind it, just a couple of players:

- **Daboochy** built the site and keeps it running: the calculator, the projection engine behind it, and the banner and event data it runs on.
- **Daptrius** made the resource spreadsheet this site grew out of. Much of the maths and data behind the numbers comes from that work.

Corrections and bug reports do get read and acted on.

## What it costs, and what it asks of you

- It is free and always has been. Hosting is paid for by Patreon supporters, and by advertising if and when it runs.
- No account is needed. The whole calculator works as a guest. Signing in only lets you save a plan and pick it up on another device.
- If you do sign in, we hold no email address, no real name and no password. The [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy) lists what is stored, why, and how to delete it.
