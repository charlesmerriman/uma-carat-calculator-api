Each uma first shows up in a limited Legend Race event. It runs for a few days, you get a few races a day, and then it's gone. Later on, usually with an anniversary, a batch of umas joins the Daily Legend Races and stays there.

In the daily races every uma has her own race, once a day. You can run all of them every day, so you can grind as many umas at once as you like.
